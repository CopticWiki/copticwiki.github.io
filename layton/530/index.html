530 The Coptic relative tense system is illustrated by the following examples.

  1. Relative time subordinate to future tense
    1. anticipated time/general potentiality
      • John 16:13
        ϥ-ⲛⲁ-ⲧⲁⲙⲱ-ⲧⲛ̄ ⲉ-ⲛ-ⲉⲧ∅-ⲛⲁ-ϣⲱⲡⲉ
        “He will declare to you the things that are to come”
      • Mark 4:24
        ϩⲙ-ⲡ̄ϣⲓ ⲉⲧⲉⲧⲛⲁ-ϣⲓ ⲙ̄ⲙⲟ-ϥ ⲥⲉ-ⲛⲁ-ϣⲓ ⲛⲏ-ⲧⲛ̄
        “With whatever measure you measure out (general potentiality), you will be measured”
    2. simultaneous time/general truth
      • Mark 11:23
        ⲡ-ⲉⲧϥ̄-ϫⲱ ⲙ̄ⲙⲟ-ϥ ϥ-ⲛⲁ-ϣⲱⲡⲉ ⲛⲁ-ϥ
        “What he says will come to pass for him”
    3. previous time
      • John 3:4
        ⲛ̄ⲁϣ ⲛ̄-ϩⲉ ⲥⲉ-ⲛⲁ-ϫⲡⲉ-ⲡⲣⲱⲙⲉ ⲛ̄ⲕⲉⲥⲟⲡ ⲉ-ⲁϥ-ⲣ̄-∅ϩⲗⲗⲟ
        “How will a person be born again, once having become old?”
  2. Relative time subordinate to present tense
    1. anticipated time/general potentiality
      • Matt 5:21
        ⲡ-ⲉⲧ∅-ⲛⲁ-ϩⲱⲧⲃ ⲇⲉ ϥ-ⲟ ⲛ̄-∅ⲉⲛⲟⲭⲟⲥ ⲉ-ⲧⲉⲕⲣⲓⲥⲓⲥ
        “Whoever kills is liable to judgement”
    2. simultaneous time/general truth
      • Matt 10:40
        ⲡ-ⲉⲧ∅-ϣⲱⲡ ⲙ̄ⲙⲱ-ⲧⲛ̄ ⲉⲣⲟ-ϥ ⲉϥ-ϣⲱⲡ ⲙ̄ⲙⲟ-ⲓ ⲉⲣⲟ-ϥ
        “The one who receives you (In receiving you one) receives me”
    3. previous time
      • Mark 6:2
        ⲟⲩ ⲧⲉ ⲧⲁⲓ̈ⲥⲟⲫⲓⲁ ⲛ̄ⲧ-ⲁⲩ-ⲧⲁⲁ-ⲥ ⲙ̄-ⲡⲁⲓ̈
        “What is this wisdom that has been given to this person?”
  3. Relative time subordinate to past tense
    1. anticipated time/general potentiality
      • Heb 11:21
        ⲓ̈ⲁⲕⲱⲃ ⲉϥ-ⲛⲁ-ⲙⲟⲩ ⲁϥ-ⲥⲙⲟⲩ ⲉ-ⲡⲟⲩⲁ ⲡⲟⲩⲁ ⲛ̄-ⲛ̄ϣⲏⲣⲉ ⲛ̄-ⲓ̈ⲱⲥⲏⲫ
        “Jacob, when he was about to die, blesed each of the sons of Joseph”
    2. simultaneous time/general truth
      • John 13:1
        ⲉϥ-ⲥⲟⲟⲩⲛ ⲛ̄ϭⲓ-ⲓ︤ⲥ︥ ϫⲉ-ⲁ-ⲧⲉϥⲟⲩⲛⲟⲩ ⲉ͡ⲓ…ⲁϥ-ⲙⲉⲣⲓⲧ-ⲟⲩ ϣⲁⲃⲟⲗ
        “When Jesus knew that his hour had come…he loved them to the end”
      • Luke 2:33
        ⲡⲉϥⲉⲓⲱⲧ ⲇⲉ ⲛⲙ̄-ⲧⲉϥⲙⲁⲁⲩ ⲛⲉⲩ-ⲣ̄-∅ϣⲡⲏⲣⲉ ⲉϫⲛ̄-ⲛ-ⲉⲧⲟⲩ-ϫⲱ ⲙ̄ⲙⲟ-ⲟⲩ ⲉⲧⲃⲏⲏⲧ-ϥ
        “And his father and his mother marveled at what was being said about him”
    3. previous time
      • Mark 7:8
        ⲉ-ⲁⲧⲉⲧⲛ̄-ⲕⲱ ⲛ̄ⲥⲱ-ⲧⲛ̄ ⲛ̄-ⲧⲉⲛⲧⲟⲗⲏ ⲙ̄-ⲡⲛⲟⲩⲧⲉ ⲧⲉⲧⲛ̄-ⲁⲙⲁϩⲧⲉ ⲛ̄-ⲙ̄ⲡⲁⲣⲁⲇⲟⲥⲓⲥ ⲛ̄-ⲣ̄ⲣⲱⲙⲉ
        “Having left the commandment of God, you hold fast to the tradition of human beings”
  4. Relative time (simultaneous and previous) subordinate to anterior future tense: rare
    1. simultaneous time/general truth
      • Acts 16:27
        ⲁϥ-ⲉⲓ ⲉϥ-ⲛⲁ-ϩⲟⲧⲃ-ⲉϥ ⲉϥ-ⲙⲉⲉⲩⲉ ϫⲉ-ⲁ-ⲛ-ⲉⲧ∅-ⲙⲏⲣ ⲡⲱⲧ ⲉⲃⲟⲗ
        “He was about to kill himself, supposing that the prisoners had escaped”
    2. previous time
      • Acts 23:12–13
        ϩⲟⲉⲓⲛⲉ ⲛ̄-ⲛ̄ⲓ̈ⲟⲩⲇⲁⲓ̈ ⲁⲩ-ⲱⲣⲕ̄ ⲉ-ⲛⲉⲩⲉⲣⲏⲩ…ⲛ-ⲉⲛⲧ-ⲁⲩ-ⲥⲙⲓⲛⲉ ⲇⲉ ⲙ̄-ⲡⲉⲓ̈ⲁⲛⲁϣ ⲛⲉⲩ-ⲛⲁ-ⲣ-ϩⲟⲩⲟ ⲉ-ϩⲙⲉ ⲛ̄-ⲣⲱⲙⲉ ⲡⲉ
        “Some of the Jews bound themselves by an oath…There were more than forty people who had made this oath”
  5. Relative time (simultaneous and previous) subordinate to anterior past tense: rare
    1. simultaneous time/general truth
      • John 5:13
        ⲓ︤ⲥ︥ ⲅⲁⲣ ⲛⲉ-ⲁϥ-ⲥⲉϩⲧ-ϥ̄ ⲉⲣⲉ-ⲟⲩⲙⲏⲏϣⲉ ϩⲙ̄-ⲡⲙⲁ ⲉⲧ∅-ⲙ̄ⲙⲁⲩ
        “For Jesus had withdrawn, as there was a crowd in that place”
    2. previous time
      • John 6:17
        ⲛⲉ-ⲁ-ⲡⲕⲁⲕⲉ ⲇⲉ ϣⲱⲡⲉ ⲉ-ⲙⲡⲉ-ⲓ︤ⲥ︥ ⲉ͡ⲓ ϣⲁⲣⲟ-ⲟⲩ
        “It had gotten dark, without Jesus having come to them”
  6. Relative time subordinate to a command, a tenseless nexus (ϣⲁⲣⲉ-, ⲛ̄ⲧⲉ-), or a verbal noun (infinitive, ⲧⲣⲉ-)
    1. anticipated time/general potentiality
      • Mark 6:11
        ⲉⲧⲉⲧⲛ-ⲛⲁ-ⲉⲓ ⲉⲃⲟⲗ ϩⲙ-ⲡⲙⲁ ⲉⲧ∅-ⲙⲙⲁⲩ ⲛⲟⲩϩⲉ ⲉⲃⲟⲗ ⲙ-ⲡϣⲟⲉⲓϣ ⲉⲧ∅-ϩⲁⲣⲟ-ⲟⲩ ⲛ-ⲛⲉⲧⲛⲟⲩⲉⲣⲏⲧⲉ
        “When you are going to leave (Whenever you leave) that place, shake off the dust that is on your feet”
    2. simultaneous time
      • Athanasius, Life of St. Anthony 5 (Garitte 8:20–22)
        ⲛⲧⲟϥ ⲇⲉ ⲉϥ-ⲙⲉⲉⲩⲉ ⲉ-ⲡⲉⲭ︤ⲥ︥ ϩⲙ-ⲡⲉϥϩⲏⲧ ⲙⲛ-ⲡⲉⲓⲱⲣϩ̄ ⲛ-ⲛⲟⲏⲣⲟⲛ ⲛ̄-ⲧⲉϥⲯⲩⲭⲏ ϣⲁϥ-ⲱϣⲙ̄ ⲛ-ⲛⲉϫⲏⲃⲥ̄ ⲙ̄-ⲡ-ⲉⲧ∅-ⲙⲙⲁⲩ
        “But he, by thinking on Christ in his heart and the intellectual vision of his soul, used to extinguish that enemy's glowing coals” (ϣⲁϥ- main-clause aorist in a past narrative context)
    3. previous time
      • Life of St. Pachomius (Lefort 103a: 1–4)
        ⲉ-∅ⲧⲣⲉϥ-ⲁⲥⲕⲉⲓ ⲛ̄ϩⲏⲧ-ϥ ϩⲙ̄-ⲡϯⲙⲉ ⲉⲧ∅-ⲟ ⲛ̄-∅ϫⲁⲓ̈ⲉ ⲛ̄ⲧ-ⲁⲩ-ⲧⲁⲙⲟ-ϥ ⲉⲧⲃⲏⲏⲧ-ϥ̄ ϩⲓⲧⲙ̄-ⲡⲛⲟⲩⲧⲉ
        “To live an ascetic life there in the deserted village about which he had been instructed by God”

Several levels of relative time can be expressed in a single sentence, as one subordinate clause relates to another subordinate clause; e.g.

  • John 13:1
    ⲉϥ-ⲥⲟⲟⲩⲛ ⲛ̄ϭⲓ-ⲓ︤ⲥ︥ ϫⲉ-ⲁ-ⲧⲉϥⲟⲩⲛⲟⲩ ⲉ͡ⲓ…ⲉ-ⲁϥ-ⲙⲉⲣⲉ-ⲛ-ⲉⲧⲉ-ⲛⲟⲩ-ϥ ⲛⲉ ⲉⲧ∅-ϩⲙ̄-ⲡⲕⲟⲥⲙⲟⲥ ⲁϥ-ⲙⲉⲣⲓⲧ-ⲟⲩ ϣⲁⲃⲟⲗ
    “When Jesus knew that His hour had come, having loved His own who were in the world, He loved them to the end”
